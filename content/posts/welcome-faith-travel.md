Faith travel succeeds when logistics protect the **why** behind your trip—not the other way around.

Here is the workflow we anchor on inside TourWise AI:

1. Decide your spine first (sites, ceremonies, walks).
2. Add buffer days—jet lag kills reverence faster than crowded museums.
3. Only then tighten affiliate-backed bookings knowing the dates are stable.

Whenever you leave this notebook for partner links, we route clicks through `/api/affiliate-click`
so budgets stay predictable and disclosures stay centralized.
