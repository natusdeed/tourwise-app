AI is most useful when you give it clear constraints.

Use this sequence:

1. Set your destination and exact trip length.
2. Define your budget level and daily budget.
3. Add traveler type and interests.
4. Generate your plan with the [AI planner](/ai-travel-planner).
5. Compare essentials in the [travel deals hub](/travel-deals).

If your budget is tight, ask for free attractions, public transport, and walking-friendly routing.  
If your budget is premium, ask for optional upgrades and private transfers.

For flight-first planning, start in the [cheap flights section](/cheap-flights).
